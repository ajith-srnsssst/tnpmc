Welfare & Protection Schemes

  • 24/7 Free Legal Aid: Direct advocate counsel against wrongful detention, defamation cases, and reporting obstructions.
  • Emergency Medical Relief: Financial grants (up to ₹50,000) for journalists injured on field duty.
  • Family Support & Education Fund: Scholarships and welfare aid for families of deceased media persons.
  • Government Accreditation: Guidance on DIPR state and district press accreditation passes.
